# Project Brief — Japan Business Directory (Frontend)

## Vision

A clean, modern, API-driven business directory for Japan that helps people discover, evaluate, and contact businesses in seconds. The product should embody "beauty with brains" — visually striking, with a distinct Japanese cultural identity, yet engineered around a frictionless user flow where every control behaves predictably and every screen serves a clear purpose.

## Goal

Build the frontend of a business directory website (consuming a listings API) that showcases a wide range of businesses across Japan and makes them effortless to find and contact.

## Core Concept

Every business listing is built around the essentials a visitor needs to take action:

- **Identity & contact:** business name, address, and phone number
- **Location:** geolocation / map placement so clients can physically find and reach the business
- **Availability:** operating hours and quick-contact options
- **Trust:** customer reviews displayed on listings — a hallmark of a quality directory
- **Tiered features:** certain listing features may vary based on the business's pricing or subscription plan

The directory is fully searchable by **keyword**, **category**, and **geolocation**.

## Primary Use Cases

1. Finding suppliers or service providers
2. Helping listed businesses gain visibility and win new clients
3. Quick lookups — e.g., finding a company's phone number or address in seconds

## UX Principles (Non-Negotiable)

- **Frictionless flow:** users reach the information they need in the fewest possible steps — no dead ends, no unnecessary clicks
- **Standardized direction:** controls, cards, buttons, and navigation patterns are consistent across the entire site, so users never have to relearn an interaction

## Landing Page Structure (top to bottom)

1. **Hero Section** — full-width static background image blending Japanese cultural elements with modern infrastructure and business life; bold headline and supporting copy over the image
2. **Search Section** — prominent search bar with a keyword field ("What are you looking for?"), a location field with geolocation icon, a category dropdown, and a red Search button; "Popular Searches" chips displayed beneath *(per reference image)*
3. **Featured Categories Section** — an icon grid answering "What do you need to find?" with categories such as Businesses & Trades, Health & Medical, Education, Food & Beverages, Digital Media & IT, Shops & Marts, Shopping & Malls, Real Estate, Arts & Entertainment, Services, and Transport & Logistics *(per reference image)*
4. **Connect With More Customers Section** — split layout pitched at business owners: persuasive copy and a strong CTA button on the left, device-framed lifestyle imagery on the right *(per reference image)*
5. **Popular Categories Section** — highlights the most-browsed categories to guide undecided visitors
6. **Why Choose Us Section** — overlapping image composition on the left; heading with three stacked value-proposition points on the right *(per reference layout)*
7. **Recent Articles Section** — one large featured article card at the center, surrounded by smaller article/blog cards *(per reference layout)*
8. **Footer Section** — standard directory footer with quick links, category links, contact details, and social icons

## Visual Identity

- **Primary color:** `#8F1D21` (deep red) for buttons, icons, headings, and accents
- **Background:** white — **Text:** black
- **Imagery direction:** a consistent aesthetic fusing Japanese culture with modern business and infrastructure, applied across the Hero, Why Choose Us, and Recent Articles visuals so the entire site feels cohesive and on-theme
- **Tone:** elegant, trustworthy, contemporary — beauty with brains

## Summary Statement

A frictionless, standardized, red-white-and-black directory experience that lets anyone in Japan find a business, its location, and its contact details in moments — while giving listed businesses a beautiful, credible stage to attract new customers.
